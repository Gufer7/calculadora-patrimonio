How does the savings calculator work?

How does a savings calculator work?

A savings calculator estimates how much money you could accumulate over time by combining your initial savings, regular contributions and an estimated interest rate.

Enter the amount you already have saved, how much you plan to add each month, the expected annual interest rate and the number of years you want to save. The calculator then estimates your future balance and separates the money you contributed from the estimated interest earned.

The calculation uses monthly compounding. This means that interest is added to the balance over time, allowing future interest to be calculated on both your original savings and previously accumulated interest.

This savings calculator estimates how your money could grow when you make regular monthly contributions and earn interest over time.

The calculation uses monthly compounding. This means that interest earned can itself generate additional interest during future months.

Why save regularly?

Making regular contributions can help build savings gradually and allows compound growth to work over longer periods of time.

How to grow your savings

The amount you can save over time depends on several factors, including your starting balance, monthly contributions, interest rate and the amount of time your money remains in the account.

Increasing your monthly contribution can have a significant effect on your final balance, especially when you continue saving for many years. A higher interest rate can also increase your savings through compound growth.

Try different combinations of contributions, interest rates and time periods above to compare different savings scenarios.

Example

If you start with $1,000, contribute $200 every month and earn an average annual interest rate of 4% for 10 years, the calculator estimates the potential value of your savings at the end of the period.

Actual results may vary depending on interest rates, account fees, taxes, inflation, and the timing of deposits.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much will my savings grow in 10 years?

It depends on your starting balance, monthly contributions and interest rate. Use the calculator above to enter your own numbers and estimate your future savings balance.

Does this savings calculator include compound interest?

Yes. The calculator applies the estimated annual interest rate monthly, so the balance can grow through compound interest over time.

How much should I save each month?

There is no single amount that works for everyone. A useful approach is to choose a monthly contribution that fits your income, expenses and savings goals, and then adjust it as your financial situation changes.

Can I use this calculator for long-term savings?

Yes. You can enter different time periods to compare how regular contributions and compound interest could affect your savings over the long term.
