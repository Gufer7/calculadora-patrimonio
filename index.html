Why use a financial calculator?

Regular saving and investing can help you build wealth over the long term. Financial calculators make it easier to explore different scenarios and understand how factors such as time, contributions and investment returns can affect your results.

The results provided by these tools are estimates and do not guarantee future investment returns.
