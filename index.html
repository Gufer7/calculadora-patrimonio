How our financial calculators can help

Financial calculators can make it easier to explore the potential effect of different financial assumptions. Changing the amount you save, the time you invest or the expected return can produce very different results over the long term.

Our Savings Calculator focuses on regular saving and estimated interest. The Compound Interest Calculator shows how an initial investment and recurring contributions could benefit from compound growth.

The Investment Calculator allows you to explore potential investment growth while accounting for expected returns, investment fees and inflation. The Net Worth Calculator provides a snapshot of your current financial position by comparing your assets and liabilities.

Compare different financial scenarios

You can use the calculators to compare different assumptions rather than relying on a single estimate. For example, you could change your monthly contribution, investment period or expected return and see how the estimated result changes.

This can help you understand the relationship between time, contributions, interest, investment growth, fees and inflation. The results are estimates based on the information you enter and are intended for educational and informational purposes.
