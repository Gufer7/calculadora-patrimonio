How Financial Calculators Can Help

Financial calculators make it easier to explore the potential effect of different financial assumptions. Changing the amount you save, the time you invest, the interest rate or the expected return can produce significantly different results over the long term.

A savings calculation can help you understand how regular contributions may accumulate over time. A compound interest calculation focuses on how an initial amount and recurring contributions can potentially grow through compounding.

Investment calculations can help you explore different return, contribution, fee and inflation assumptions. Mortgage calculations can help you estimate monthly payments and understand how interest and loan terms affect the overall cost of borrowing.

The Net Worth Calculator provides a snapshot of your financial position by comparing your assets with your liabilities.

Compare Different Financial Scenarios

Financial planning often involves comparing several scenarios rather than relying on a single estimate. For example, you could compare different monthly contributions, investment periods, mortgage rates, loan terms or expected investment returns.

Comparing scenarios can help you understand the relationship between time, contributions, interest, investment growth, mortgage costs, fees and inflation. The calculators are designed to make these comparisons easier to explore.
