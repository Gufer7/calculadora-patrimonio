What Is Compound Interest?

Compound interest allows the returns generated by an investment to be reinvested and potentially generate additional returns. Over time, this effect can significantly increase the growth of your investment.

For example, if you invest money and earn an annual return, future returns can be generated not only from your original investment but also from the returns accumulated over time.

This calculator is provided for informational and educational purposes. Actual investment returns can vary, and past or estimated returns do not guarantee future results.
