How does an investment calculator work?

An investment calculator estimates how your money could grow when an initial investment is combined with regular contributions and an assumed annual return.

The calculation compounds the estimated return over the selected investment period. Monthly contributions are added throughout the period.

This calculator also allows you to include estimated investment fees and inflation so you can see how these assumptions can affect the long-term result.

Why investment fees matter

Investment fees may appear small when viewed over a single year, but their effect can become more significant over long periods because money paid in fees is no longer available to compound.

For example, an investment with a 7% expected annual return and 0.2% annual fees is modeled using a net return of approximately 6.8% before considering inflation.

Actual investment costs can be more complicated and may include fund expenses, platform fees, trading costs, taxes or other charges that are not included in this calculator.

Why inflation matters

Inflation reduces the purchasing power of money over time. Therefore, a future investment balance may have less purchasing power than the same amount of money today.

The inflation-adjusted result shown by this calculator is only an estimate. Actual inflation can change from year to year.

Example investment scenario

Suppose you invest $5,000 initially and contribute $200 every month. If you assume a 7% annual return, 0.2% annual fees and 2% annual inflation over 20 years, the calculator estimates how your investment could develop under those assumptions.

The result should be viewed as a mathematical projection rather than a prediction of future market performance.

Frequently Asked Questions

What return should I use?

The appropriate assumption depends on the type of investment and the period being considered. Historical returns do not guarantee future results, so it can be useful to compare several different scenarios rather than relying on a single assumption.

Does this calculator guarantee investment returns?

No. Investment returns are uncertain. This calculator only produces a mathematical estimate based on the assumptions you enter.

Does the calculator include investment fees?

Yes. You can enter an estimated annual investment fee and the calculator subtracts it from the expected annual return.

Does it account for inflation?

Yes. The calculator provides an estimated inflation-adjusted value to illustrate the potential effect of inflation on future purchasing power.

Can I use this calculator for stocks or ETFs?

You can use it for educational projections involving investments such as stocks, ETFs or diversified portfolios. However, actual returns, fees, taxes and volatility can differ substantially from the assumptions entered.
