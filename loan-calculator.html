How does a loan calculator work?

A loan calculator estimates the regular payment required to repay a loan over a specified period. The calculation uses the amount borrowed, the interest rate and the repayment term.

With an amortizing loan, each payment normally includes an interest portion and a principal portion. As the balance decreases, the amount of interest charged can also decrease over time.

The results can help you compare different hypothetical loan scenarios and understand how the interest rate and repayment term affect borrowing costs.

How does the interest rate affect a loan?

A higher interest rate generally increases the monthly payment and the total interest paid over the life of the loan.

Comparing different assumed interest rates can show how borrowing costs may change over the same repayment period.

How does the loan term affect the total cost?

A longer repayment term can reduce the required monthly payment but may increase the total amount of interest paid.

A shorter term can require higher monthly payments while reducing the amount of time interest is charged.

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of loans can I calculate?

This calculator can be used for general amortizing loan scenarios, such as personal loans, auto loans and other installment loans. Actual loan terms vary by lender and product.

Does this calculator include loan fees?

No. The calculator estimates principal and interest only. Origination fees, application fees, insurance and other charges are not included.

Does a lower interest rate reduce the monthly payment?

Generally, yes. For the same loan amount and repayment term, a lower interest rate generally results in a lower monthly payment and lower total interest.

Does a longer loan term reduce the monthly payment?

Generally, yes. Extending the repayment term can reduce the monthly payment, but it can also increase the total interest paid.

Are the results guaranteed?

No. The results are estimates based on the assumptions entered. Actual loan costs depend on the lender, loan agreement, interest rate, fees and other conditions.

Can I compare different loan scenarios?

Yes. You can change the loan amount, interest rate and repayment term to compare different hypothetical borrowing scenarios.
