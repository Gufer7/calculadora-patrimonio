What Is Compound Interest?

Compound interest is the process of earning returns on your original investment as well as on returns that have already accumulated. When returns remain invested, the accumulated balance can generate additional returns over time.

The potential effect of compound growth generally becomes more noticeable over longer periods. The amount invested, the assumed rate of return, the timing of contributions and the time invested can all affect the final value.

How to Calculate Compound Interest

A basic compound interest formula for a lump-sum investment is:

A = P(1 + r/n)nt

In this formula, A is the final amount, P is the initial principal, r is the annual interest rate, n is the number of compounding periods per year and t is the investment period in years.

When regular monthly contributions are included, the calculation also needs to account for the amount and timing of each contribution. This calculator uses a monthly calculation to estimate the future investment value.

Why Does Time Matter With Compound Interest?

Time can have a significant effect on compound growth because returns that remain invested can generate additional returns in later periods. As the investment period becomes longer, the compounding effect can become increasingly important.

Regular contributions can also increase the amount of money that remains invested and potentially benefits from future growth.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the compound interest formula?

A basic compound interest formula for a lump-sum investment is A = P(1 + r/n)nt . When regular contributions are included, the calculation also needs to account for the amount and timing of those contributions.

How does compound interest work with monthly contributions?

Each monthly contribution increases the amount invested. If returns are reinvested, the accumulated balance can continue generating additional returns over time.

Does this calculator include monthly contributions?

Yes. You can enter a regular monthly contribution to estimate how recurring investments could affect the future value of your investment.

What is a good annual return to use?

There is no guaranteed annual return for an investment. You can use different assumptions to compare scenarios, but actual investment returns vary over time and can also be negative.

Does compound interest guarantee investment growth?

No. This calculator uses an assumed annual return to produce an estimate. Actual investment returns can be higher or lower, and investments can lose value.

How long does it take for compound interest to work?

Compound growth does not have a fixed time period. Its potential effect generally becomes more noticeable when money remains invested for longer periods and returns are reinvested.
