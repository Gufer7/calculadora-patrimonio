What Is Net Worth?

Net worth is the value of your financial assets after accounting for your liabilities. Building savings and investing consistently can help increase your net worth over the long term.

This calculator estimates how your current savings and regular contributions could grow if they earned a constant annual return.

The results are estimates for educational purposes only. Investment returns are not guaranteed and actual results may vary.
