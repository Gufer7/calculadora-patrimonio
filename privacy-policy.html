Privacy Policy

Information about privacy, cookies and data processing

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Financial Calculators handles information when you visit and use this website.

Financial Calculators does not intentionally collect sensitive personal information through the financial calculators themselves. Information entered into the calculators is processed in your browser to generate the requested estimates.

When you use this website, we may receive information about website usage through Google Analytics, including information such as the number of users, session information, approximate location, browser type and device information.

If you contact us by email, we may receive the information you choose to provide in your message, such as your email address and the content of your message.

3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

This website uses Google Analytics to measure website usage and understand how visitors interact with the website. Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies to distinguish users and sessions and to provide measurement information.

Google Analytics may store an identifier in a first-party cookie called _ga when analytics storage is enabled.

Where required by applicable law, we will request consent before using cookies or similar technologies that require user consent. You can also control or delete cookies through your browser settings.

4. Third-Party Services

We use Google Analytics, provided by Google, to measure website traffic and usage. Google may process information collected through the service according to its own privacy policies and terms.

We may also use other third-party services in the future, including advertising services such as Google AdSense. If such services are added, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe their use and the applicable privacy and consent requirements.

5. Financial Calculators

The information entered into the calculators is used to generate estimates within your browser. Calculator results are for informational and educational purposes only.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including rights to access, correct or delete certain information.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the website, services or applicable requirements.
